## What the import could not express (gaps)

- **hard per-stub cap (provared.order.place each 100 GBP).** AMEP limits can all be lifted by an approval that passed I8; a Provared `each` cap cannot. Not mapped; an approved stub above it would be preserved in AMEP but over-limit in Provared
- **count limit (provared.message.send count 2 per 3600 s).** AMEP limits cap amounts in a unit, not numbers of actions. Not mapped
- **rules of conduct.** never: destroys, impersonate, bypass. Kinds of action are enforced by the mapped actions list; rules of conduct cannot be checked from records (Provared says so too, section 13)
- **origin of the AMEP objects.** AMEP objects are signed by the importer's keys after the profile verified the Provared signatures (passkey, Ed25519 and ML-DSA-87). AMEP cannot yet verify passkey or ML-DSA signatures itself, so the mandate, invocations and approvals are re-expressions and receipts are capped at L1
