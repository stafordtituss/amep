AMEP is a protocol for the paper trail of an AI agent's work. Every grant, hand-off and action is signed by the party that made it. Anyone holding the records can check, offline, whether what happened stayed inside what a person allowed, and get the same answer as everyone else.

I · Mandate

Authority, with its bounds written down.

A person signs what an agent may do: which actions, on which resources, up to which effect class, until when. Each hand-off is a new sheet, signed by the one handing over, and it can only be narrower than the sheet beneath it.

I1

Only narrows. A delegation that widens actions, resources, data, ceiling or time is a break in the chain, whoever signed it.

I6

Still in force. Authority must be live when each effect happens, not only when the call was made. Revocation counts from its own time.

I8

Approvals are exact. A human approval covers one exact effect, or the exact effects of one operation, once. A retry can't spend it again.

I2

Effects stay inside. What actually executed must fall within every sheet in the stack, and within the service the authority names.

II · Evidence

Signed by whoever saw it happen.

Each record is an exhibit with a signer and a digest. The last one, what was actually done, has to come from someone other than the agent's host, because an agent's own account of a high-impact action isn't evidence of it.

III · Verdict

Three answers. A gap is never a yes.

Every verdict names the rule that decided it. A violation names who departed first; an open question names the record that would settle it. Two verifiers given the same packet and keys return the same record, byte for byte.

Preserved

The effect landed inside every boundary in the chain.

Violated

It crossed a boundary. The result names the invariant, such as I2 or I8, and the party to blame.

Unresolved

A record is missing. The result names it, such as receipt or held_outcome, and a retry waits until it's known.
